# Social Post — one idea, written for the network it will live on

The same message reads differently on LinkedIn and on X, and a post that
ignores where it lives gets scrolled past. This skill writes one post for one
network: the hook, the body, the ask, the format and length that network
rewards — in the voice the brand already has — and gives the person a choice
of variants, never a single take-it-or-leave-it draft.

## Related skills
- **voice-guide** — how the brand sounds; read it before writing a word.
- **repurpose-content** — when the starting point is an article or a talk
  and the job is several derivatives.
- **content-plan** — what to post when; this skill writes one slot.
- **proofread-edit** — the last pass on a post that matters.
(If a referenced skill is not installed, do the equivalent inline.)

## When to use
- Any single post, thread or caption for a named network.
- **Do NOT use** to post anything. Writing is this skill; publishing is a
  message in the person's name and needs their yes and their account.

## Workflow
1. **One idea.** What should the reader think, feel or do after this post?
   If the request carries three ideas, it is three posts — say so.
2. **Read the voice.** The brand's voice guide if there is one; otherwise
   two or three of their recent posts on this network.
3. **Write to the network** (see Formats). The first line carries the post:
   a specific claim, a number, a tension, a question the reader has — never
   "Excited to announce".
4. **Write two or three variants** that differ in the hook or the angle, not
   in synonyms. Label what each tries.
5. **Check** against the Standards, count the characters, and hand them over
   with the one you would pick and why.

## Formats
- **LinkedIn** — hook in the first ~200 characters (before "see more"),
  short paragraphs, one story or one lesson, a question or a soft ask at the
  end; 3 hashtags at most; links cost reach — offer to put one in the first
  comment.
- **X** — one post ≤ 280 characters with the point in the first line, or a
  thread whose first post stands alone and each next post adds one step;
  1–2 hashtags or none.
- **Instagram** — the image or video carries it; the caption's first line
  explains why to stop; line breaks; hashtags at the end, a handful that the
  audience actually follows; say what the visual should show.
- **Threads / Facebook** — conversational, shorter than LinkedIn, a question
  that invites replies.
- **TikTok / Reels caption** — one line that adds what the video does not
  say; the hook belongs in the first second of the video, so suggest it.

## Standards
- One idea per post. A post that tries to say everything says nothing.
- Specific beats clever: a real number, a real customer (with their
  permission), a real before-and-after.
- No engagement bait ("comment YES"), no fake urgency, no claims the product
  cannot back.
- Never tag or name a person or company without the person's say-so.
- Accessibility: alt text for every image, no meaning carried by emoji alone,
  CamelCase for multi-word hashtags.

## Output
For each variant: the network, the post as it would appear, the character
count, what the variant tries. Then: the one you recommend and why, the
visual it needs (if any), and the best time to post if the person has told
you their audience's hours.

## Defaults
- No network named → ask once; if there is no answer, LinkedIn for B2B,
  Instagram for consumer.
- No voice guide → mirror their recent posts and say that is what you did.
- Asked for "a few posts" → three, each a different idea, one variant each.
